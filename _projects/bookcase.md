I built this 7-foot bookcase to hold my books and vinyl records, and designed it to be flexible as the collection changes.

## Design

The bookcase is split into three vertical sections, with a solid red oak face frame that gives the plywood a finished, furniture-quality look. Every shelf is adjustable, so the spacing can change with what's on it. Right now, vinyl records sit on the bottom shelves, where they get the extra height they need.

I built the carcass from red oak plywood, assembling the sides, top, bottom, and two vertical dividers with pocket screws and glue. A solid red oak face frame covers the plywood edges along the front, and the shelves rest on adjustable supports so they can move as needed.

For the finish, I chose Mahogany stain for a rich, warm reddish-brown, then sealed it with polyurethane to protect the shelves from the wear of books and records going in and out.
