Hello! I’m Annie Branum-Jiao, a data engineer who loves solving puzzles by building data systems and designing beautiful dashboards that people actually use. I build ETL/ELT pipelines with Snowflake, DBT cloud, semantic layers for self-service and agentic AI analytics, and dashboards in Power BI and Tableau.

I hold an MS in Data Science from UT Austin (4.0 GPA) and a BS in Physics. Before moving into data engineering, I spent six years in biomedical research, where I learned to be rigorous about where numbers come from and what they really mean. I bring that same care to every model and metric I build.

When I’m not wrangling data, I’m usually designing something, usually a quilt, woodworking project, or painting. I have two Tonkinese kitties and love watching trashy reality TV.
